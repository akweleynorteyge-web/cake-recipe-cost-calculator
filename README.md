# 🎂 Cake Recipe Cost Calculator - Ghana

A simple, web-based cost calculator for cake bakers in Ghana. Calculate your cake costs in GH₵ (Ghana cedis) with ease!

## Features

### 1. **Ingredient Database**
- Add, edit, and delete ingredients
- Categorize ingredients (dry, wet, decorations, packaging)
- Track purchase price, quantity, and units (kg, g, litre, ml, cup, tablespoon, teaspoon, each)
- All data saved in your browser's local storage

### 2. **Recipe Calculator**
- Select ingredients from your database
- Enter the amount used in your recipe
- Automatic cost calculation based on purchase price
- Easy ingredient removal from recipes

### 3. **Cake Costs**
- Add costs for:
  - Packaging
  - Electricity
  - Gas
  - Water
  - Labour
  - Transport
  - Decorations
- Set profit percentage

### 4. **Packaging Management**
Set prices for different sizes:
- 6-inch, 8-inch, 10-inch, 12-inch boxes
- 8-inch, 10-inch, 12-inch cake boards
- Cake drum
- Ribbon
- Dowels

### 5. **Decorations**
Add and manage decoration costs:
- Gold leaf
- Fresh flowers
- Topper
- Fondant
- Wafer paper flowers
- Edible image
- Sprinkles
- Sugar balls
- Food colouring
- And more!

### 6. **Cost Summary**
Visualize your complete cost breakdown:
- Total ingredient cost
- Decoration cost
- Packaging cost
- Labour
- Utilities (Electricity + Gas + Water)
- Transport
- **Total production cost**
- **Profit**
- **Final selling price**

## How to Use

1. **Open the app** in your web browser
2. **Go to Ingredients tab** - Add all your ingredients with their costs
3. **Go to Recipe tab** - Create a new recipe by selecting ingredients and quantities
4. **Go to Packaging tab** - Set your packaging prices (boxes, boards, etc.)
5. **Go to Decorations tab** - Add your decoration items and costs
6. **Go to Cost Summary tab** - Enter other costs and set your profit margin
7. **View your final selling price!**

## Installation

Simply download or clone this repository and open `index.html` in your web browser. No installation or server required!

```bash
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/cake-recipe-cost-calculator.git
cd cake-recipe-cost-calculator
# Open index.html in your browser
```

## Data Storage

All your data is stored locally in your browser using LocalStorage. This means:
- ✅ Your data stays private
- ✅ Works offline
- ✅ No internet required
- ⚠️ Data is lost if you clear browser cache/cookies

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Build your ingredient database first** - Add all ingredients you commonly use
2. **Keep prices updated** - Update ingredient costs as prices change
3. **Test different profit margins** - See how different percentages affect your final price
4. **Save multiple recipes** - Create variations for different cake sizes
5. **Export your costs** - Take screenshots for your records
